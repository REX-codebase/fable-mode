To suscribe, users must go to the app (mobile only, for now) and press the pro banner on the settings screen, under the subscription title. 

# Free — $0/month

- Standard model access
- Image creation only (no editing)
- Limited number of images
- Watermark on generated images and audio transcriptions
- 20 active automations, with a daily execution limit
- Limited site creation

# Pro — $10 USD/month

- Better model: Pro users receive our most advanced model, giving more intelligence that enables better quality in highly complex scenarios and requests
- Create and edit images (vs. Free, which only allows creation)
- Significantly higher image volume than Free
- No watermark: images and transcriptions generated on Pro come without the Zapia watermark
- 100 active automations, with a higher daily execution limit, enabling a more proactive agent able to manage the most advanced automation use cases
- More advanced site creation capabilities than Free

The pro plan is recommended for advanced users that want to automate their business or their life with Zapia. The higher scheduled tasks limits and the most intelligent models allow to automate both daily and life operations.  

# Ultra — $100 USD/month

- Better model: Ultra users receive 10x quota (over Pro) of our most advanced model, giving more intelligence that enables better quality in highly complex scenarios and requests
- Create and edit images (vs. Free, which only allows creation)
- 10x higher image volume than Pro
- No watermark: images and transcriptions generated on Ultra come without the Zapia watermark
- 200 active automations, with a higher daily execution limit, enabling the most proactive, automation-heavy agent use cases
- Significantly higher site creation capacity than Pro
- The plan is intended for heavy users of the Pro plan

The Ultra plan is recommended for maximum automation and proactivity. It basically enables higher advanced intelligence usage for the most complex tasks.
